## Правила

**Ability checks** are a thing that you decide to do (usually on your turn). **Saving throws** are a thing the DM tells you to roll.(Usually on somebody else's turn).
isc

**Temporary hit points** represent an increase in your character's HP pool for a limited amount of time, lasting either until depleted or until your character takes a long rest unless otherwise specified.
