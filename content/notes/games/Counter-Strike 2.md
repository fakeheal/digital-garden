
# General

In practice mode, first enable cheats by:
```sh
sv_cheats 1
```

Then do:
```
bind "Alt" "noclip;"
bind "N" "sv_rethrow_last_grenade"
bind "C" "ent_fire smokegrenade_projectile kill; stopsound"
```

Pressing <kbd>Alt</kbd> toggles clip/noclip mode. <kbd>N</kbd> will rethrow last grenade (to test flashes from different angles). <kbd>C</kbd> will remove all smokes & smoke sounds from the map.


# Smokes & Flashes

## Mirage
### T-Spawn to Connector

Stand in front of the trash bin, hugging the wall with your right shoulder. Align with the middle of the door (1px to the left). Jump and throw the smoke:

![Mirage: T-Spawn -> Connector](notes/assets/c2_mirage_tspawn_connector_smoke.png)

### T-Spawn to Window
Stand in front of the trash bin, hugging the wall with your right shoulder. Align with the railing & the white part of the wall. Start sliding with "D", jump and throw the smoke:

![Mirage: T-spawn -> Window](notes/assets/cs2_mirage_tspawn_to_window_smoke.png)

### Ramp to Jungle & Connector

Stand in the corner made by the wooden pole & the wall. Align as shown in the image. Just right-click throw the smoke.

![Mirage: Ramp -> Jungle and connector](notes/assets/cs2_mirage_ramp_jump_and_connector_smoke.png)

### Ramp to A Stairs

Stand in the right side of the door frame, align with the upper corner of the tallest building, jump & throw smoke.
*note: it's a slow smoke*

![Mirage: Ramp -> Stairs](notes/assets/cs2_mirage_ramp_to_stairs_smoke.png)

### Back Alley to Market Window

Stand in the corner as pictured & align with the highest block of the corner. Jump and throw smoke:

![Mirage: Back Alley to Market Window](notes/assets/cs2_mirage_back_alley_to_market_window_smoke.png)

### Back Alley to Market Door

Stand in the corner as pictured & align with the top of the middle window. Jump and throw the smoke:

![Mirage: Back Alley to Market Door](notes/assets/cs2_mirage_back_alley_to_market_door_smoke.png)

### Window Clear

Flashing middle or throwing a grenade to peak through smoke. Crouch near the bench thingy & the wall. Aim for the dark spot on the ground, while crouching jump and right-click throw the grenade/flash.

![Mirage: Window Clear](notes/assets/cs2_mirage_window_clear_smoke_flash.png)
