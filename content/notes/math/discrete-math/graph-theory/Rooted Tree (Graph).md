A rooted [[notes/math/discrete-math/graph-theory/Tree (Graph)|tree]] is a tree with a designated root [[notes/math/discrete-math/graph-theory/Vertex|vertex]] where every [[notes/math/discrete-math/graph-theory/Edge|edge]] either points away from or towards the root node. When edges point away from the root the graph is called an **arborescence** (out-tree) and anti-arborescence (in-tree) otherwise.
