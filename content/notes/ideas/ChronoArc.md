**ChronoArc** is a tool for comic fans to organize and enjoy their reading experience. It connects to the [ComicVine](https://comicvine.gamespot.com) database, so you can easily explore and work with a huge library of comics.

Features include:

- **Create Reading Orders**: Build your own guides to organize story arcs or character journeys.
- **Subscribe to Reading Orders**: Follow lists made by others to keep track of popular or recommended reading paths.
- **Track Your Progress**: Mark issues as read and see how far you’ve come in a reading order.
- **Manage Your Account**: Save your reading orders, subscriptions, and settings in one place.
- **Review Your History**: Look back at the comics and arcs you’ve completed.

This app is something I’ve been passionate about building, and my goal is to make it simple for readers to dive into comics without feeling lost. ChronoArc is for anyone who loves comics and wants a better way to keep track of their journey.
