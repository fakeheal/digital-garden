## Features

- **Staples** - a dynamically‑updated library of “staple” cards—those must‑haves in virtually any deck for a given format;
- **Aspect Analysis/Ink Analysis, etc** - Auto‑analysed breakdowns of each card’s characteristics: mana curve, color (ink) distribution, archetype affinities, and statistical impact;
- **Current meta** - real‑time snapshot of the competitive landscape - what decks are winning, trending picks, and emerging tech cards;
- **Flashcards** - a spaced‑repetition flashcard system for learning card text, rulings, combos, and even archetype principles.
- **Guides** - Structured walkthroughs tailored to different play modes and deck archetypes, complete with strategy tips, sideboard plans, and practice drills.
